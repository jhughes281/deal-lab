        Step one

        The property

        What you are buying, how you intend to hold it, and what it costs to get in the door. Everything downstream recalculates from these figures as you type.

        Step two

        Market and location

        Location is the one input you cannot renovate. Score the market before you score the building — a clean set of numbers in a failing submarket is still a bad deal.

        Step three

        Comparables

        Value and rent both come from the market, not from a seller's spreadsheet. Enter what has actually sold and what is actually renting nearby.

        Step four

        Income and expenses

        Build this from a blank sheet rather than from the seller's numbers. Question every line, and put a management fee in even if you plan to manage it yourself.

        Step five

        Financing

        Debt does not change what a building earns — it changes what you keep. Cap rate stays put when you move these; cash-on-cash and coverage move a lot.
